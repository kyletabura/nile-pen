Dalia Mori Wanki, Founder & Editor · Kampala, Uganda

The Nile Pen was founded by Dalia Mori Wanki, a young writer based in Kampala, Uganda, who grew tired of watching stories about South Sudan and the African continent get flattened into headlines — stripped of context, nuance, and the humanity of the people living inside them.

This platform exists because young Africans — and young South Sudanese especially — deserve journalism that treats them as intelligent people. Not summaries. Not charity. Real, honest, well-crafted stories about climate, justice, and politics, written by people who are not just covering these issues from the outside, but living them.

“Young Africans deserve stories that treat them as intelligent people — not just headlines, but context, depth, and truth told from the inside.”

What makes The Nile Pen different is that it is personal. The reporting comes from inside — from writers who carry these stories in their own lives, their own families, their own futures. That closeness is not a bias. It is the whole point.

The vision is simple and ambitious: to become the platform where young African writers bring their best work, and a name that earns a seat at the table — in UN forums, at climate conferences, and in the journalism fellowships shaping the next generation of global storytelling.
